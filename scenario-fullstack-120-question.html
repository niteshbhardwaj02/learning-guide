A route through all 120.

Twelve sessions, ten scenarios each. Try your answer before revealing the solution. Revisit anything you cannot explain without the diagram.

01 / FRAMEClarify the symptom, constraints and invariant.
02 / EXPLAINDescribe the mechanism that causes the failure.
03 / FIXPropose a concrete design or code change.
04 / TRADE OFFName a limitation and a reasonable alternative.
05 / VERIFYShow how a test or metric would prove the fix.
Suggested self-score: 0 = cannot explain; 1 = names a fix; 2 = explains why it works; 3 = discusses trade-offs; 4 = includes failure tests and operational evidence. The guide does not automatically grade your answer.
